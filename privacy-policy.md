## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you give us

**Account and sign-in.** You can sign up using Google or GitHub. When you do, we receive from that provider your name, email address, profile picture and a unique account identifier. We never receive or store your Google or GitHub password.

**Onboarding and profile.** During onboarding and afterwards, we ask for:
- Your name and profile picture
- Your sex
- Your date of birth (used to confirm you are 18 or older)
- Skills you can teach, or a statement that you have none to offer right now
- Skills you want to learn
- Any bio, links, portfolio or proof of skill you choose to add

**Swap and token activity.** Swap offers you send or receive, approvals and declines, tokens you buy, hold, spend and earn, session times, cancellations, and completion confirmations.

**Messages.** Messages you send in the temporary chat that opens after a swap offer is approved, and messages you send to our support team.

**Reviews and ratings.** Ratings and written feedback you give or receive about sessions.

**Payments.** When you buy tokens, the payment is processed by our payment gateway, [PAYMENT GATEWAY NAME]. We do **not** collect or store your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking credentials. We receive confirmation details such as the transaction ID, amount, date, payment method type and status.

**Support and chatbot.** What you write to our FAQ chatbot or support team, and any files you attach.

### 2.2 Information we collect automatically

- **Device and log data:** IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, pages viewed, referring URLs, timestamps, and error logs.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** See Section 8.
- **Session metadata:** When a Google Meet session is scheduled through SkillSwap, we record the scheduled time and meeting link. We do not record the audio or video of your sessions.

### 2.3 Information from others

- Other users may give you ratings or reviews, or report you under our Terms of Service.
- Google and GitHub send us the profile information described above when you sign in with them.

### 2.4 What we do not collect

We do not knowingly collect government ID numbers, financial account numbers, or information about your health, religion, caste, or political views. Please do not put this kind of information in your profile, chat messages or reviews.
